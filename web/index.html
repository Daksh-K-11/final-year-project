Deep Learning Architecture & Mathematical Formulations

Formal theoretical specifications of the BiLSTM-Attention and Transformer pipelines.

1. Bi-directional LSTM with Temporal Attention

Forward & Backward Recurrent States: \vec{h}_t = \text{LSTM}(\vec{h}_{t-1}, x_t), \quad \overleftarrow{h}_t = \text{LSTM}(\overleftarrow{h}_{t+1}, x_t) h_t = [\vec{h}_t; \overleftarrow{h}_t] \in \mathbb{R}^{2d}
Temporal Attention Alignment & Context Vector: e_t = v_a^T \tanh(W_a h_t + b_a) \alpha_t = \frac{\exp(e_t)}{\sum_{k=1}^T \exp(e_k)}, \quad c = \sum_{t=1}^T \alpha_t h_t

Captures bidirectional temporal dependencies over the 24-hour sequence and dynamically weights critical rush hour or atmospheric accumulation periods.

2. Time-Series Transformer with Multi-Head Self-Attention

Scaled Dot-Product Attention: \text{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{Q K^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right) V
Multi-Head Aggregation: \text{MultiHead}(Q, K, V) = \text{Concat}(\text{head}_1, \dots, \text{head}_h) W^O

Computes pairwise temporal self-attention across historical time steps simultaneously, overcoming sequential bottleneck and capturing multi-scale periodicity.
