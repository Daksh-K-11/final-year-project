Deep Learning Architecture & Theoretical Formulations

Neural Specifications
Formal theoretical formulations of the recurrent, attention-based, and ensemble meta-learning pipelines.
Recurrent + Attention

1. Bi-directional LSTM with Temporal Attention

Forward & Backward Recurrent States: \vec{h}_t = \text{LSTM}(\vec{h}_{t-1}, x_t), \quad \overleftarrow{h}_t = \text{LSTM}(\overleftarrow{h}_{t+1}, x_t) h_t = [\vec{h}_t; \overleftarrow{h}_t] \in \mathbb{R}^{2d}
Temporal Attention Alignment & Context Vector: e_t = v_a^T \tanh(W_a h_t + b_a) \alpha_t = \frac{\exp(e_t)}{\sum_{k=1}^T \exp(e_k)}, \quad c = \sum_{t=1}^T \alpha_t h_t

Captures bidirectional temporal dependencies across the 24-hour sequence and dynamically weights critical rush-hour or atmospheric accumulation intervals to maximize predictive transparency.

Self-Attention Network

2. Time-Series Multi-Head Transformer

Scaled Dot-Product Attention: \text{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{Q K^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right) V
Multi-Head Self-Attention Projection: \text{MultiHead}(Q, K, V) = \text{Concat}(\text{head}_1, \dots, \text{head}_h) W^O

Computes pairwise temporal self-attention across all historical time steps simultaneously, resolving sequential recurrence bottlenecks and extracting multi-scale cyclical dynamics.

Meta-Learner Ensemble

3. Super Hybrid Stacking Meta-Learner (DL + Boosting + Bagging)

Stacking Meta-Regression with Convex Constraints: \hat{y} = \sum_{m=1}^M w_m \cdot f_m(X) + b, \quad \text{s.t. } w_m \ge 0, \; \sum w_m = 1

Integrates diverse inductive biases: multi-layer LSTM (temporal smoothness), BiLSTM-Attention (rush-hour attention), Histogram Gradient Boosting (tabular non-linear splits), and Transformer (long-range dependencies). Achieves state-of-the-art 97.67% CPCB category accuracy and MAE 3.61.
